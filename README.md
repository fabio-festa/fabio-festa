## ⭐ Featured: Pinglets

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://mekaigames.com/pinglets"><img src="assets/pinglets/banner.gif" alt="Pinglets: tiny pixel crew members walking on the Dock, one of them fighting a glitch" width="100%"></a>
</p>

<p align="center">
  <b>A tiny pixel crew member that lives on your Dock or taskbar.</b><br>
  It flies on focus missions while you work, plays games with you right on the desktop, reminds you to take breaks,
  keeps your to-dos and tidies your desktop. It even builds its own little lab.
</p>
